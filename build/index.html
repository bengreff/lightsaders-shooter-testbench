Lab checks first

  1. Sheet: measure the robot's black side-plate stock with calipers. 6.35 mm (1/4 in) = go: every acetal part is drawn for it. The decoded robot CAD says the chassis plates are 3.17-3.18 mm; the photo (IMG_1809) looks thicker. If it is 1/8 in: cut every plate twice and screw the pairs together (same outline), because a single 1/8 in side plate is about 10x softer sideways (derived: 2.1 vs 22.9 N/mm).
  2. Cut laser/coupon.dxf first in the real sheet: a 6801 must press in by hand and stay (20.9 / 21.0 / 21.1; the plates use 21.0), a 1611 the same (13.9 / 14.0 / 14.1; plates use 14.0), a 6 mm standoff turns freely in 6.1 / 6.2 / 6.3 (the pawl's pivot hole is 6.1; the slots and the pawl's track are 6.6 wide), M4 4.2 / 4.4 / 4.6 (plates use 4.4), M3 3.2 / 3.4 / 3.6. Tell me which fits and I re-cut the DXFs.
  3. Gear holes: the 6 holes v5 uses are the ones the team's deck uses today (x +-64, y +-16 and x +-16, y +64 mm): try an M4 in each before you print: they read as tapped (3.58 mm in goBILDA's CAD, and nothing under the gear could take a nut).
  4. Motor label: read the ratio on the team turret's 5203 (the CAD does not say; v5 assumes 1:1 = 6000 rpm, guessed from the fitted 5567 rpm free speed).
  5. Servo: v5's switch times assume a 25-3 Speed at 6 V. On a hub servo port (5 V) it still works, slower; 6 V needs a servo power module.

Assembly

Highlighted: the step's parts. Grey: what is already there. Frame: +u = the servo side, -u = the motor side.

3D

1. Off the robot: the team turret (only once v5 has passed its bench checks)

  • Until v5 has run on the bench (step 13), the team turret stays on the robot. Then: 8 x M4 out of the deck (they go into the 176T gear's tapped holes at x +-64, y +-16 and x +-16, y +-64 mm from the swivel axis) and lift the whole turret off. Keep the screws.
  • What stays on the robot: the 3208 turret with its 176T ring and 64T pinion, the turret plate, the turret servo and its ServoBlock, the gear protector and holder. What moves over to v5: the 5203 motor, the Hogback 96 (if you have no spare), the 1611 bearings (2 needed).

3D

2. Spigot + base piece onto the 3208

  • Drop the printed spigot down through the 3208's 105 mm bore: its flange sits on the gear's top face (snug: sand its outside if it binds).
  • Nuts first: 1 M4 hex nut into each ear's pocket (open at the ear's top) and 2 into each buttress (the tall fins on the inside of the ears: hex pockets on their inner faces; a dab of tape holds each nut until its screw catches).
  • Base piece over the spigot (its underside recess takes the flange) and onto the gear: 4 x M4 x 14 socket heads down the ears' counterbores into the gear's tapped holes at x +-64, y +-16 (the same holes the team's deck used), 2 x M4 x 10 button heads in the 2 front bosses (x +-16, y +64). 1.5 N m: they thread into aluminium, the clamp is on PETG.
  • On the bench (no spare 3208): stand the base piece on a flat board with 2 strips of 3 mm under its ears (the spigot hangs below).

3D

3. POLLEN top half (bench)

  • 4 x M3 heat-set inserts into the 4 holes in the 2 wing ends (soldering iron, 220-230 C, flush).
  • Press a 6801 bearing into each top plate (vise + a 21 mm socket on the outer race; acetal takes a press fit).
  • Pins: a 12 mm washer then a goBILDA 22 mm M4 standoff on each plate's OUTER face, an M4 x 14 socket head with an 8 mm washer from the inner face into it. Tight: this clamp carries the pin's bending.
  • Plates onto the print's wing ends, the +u plate (the one with the 40T sector) on the servo side: 2 x M3 x 10 button heads each.

3D

4. POLLEN lower half + latch pawl (bench)

  • 4 x M3 inserts into the wing ends; a 6801 into each lower plate.
  • Pawl pivot on the -u plate (motor side): goBILDA 7 mm M4 standoff on the plate's outer face, an M4 x 18 button head from the inner face through the plate, the standoff and the pawl, a 10 mm washer and an M4 nyloc beyond the pawl. Snug, then back a quarter turn: the pawl must swing freely.
  • Plates onto the wing ends: 2 x M3 x 10 button heads each. The 31 deg arc slot in each plate is for the top half's pin.

3D

5. NECTAR central tooth (bench)

  • 4 x M3 inserts into the strap's 2 end blocks; a 6801 into each NECTAR plate; plates onto the strap ends: 2 x M3 x 10 button heads each.

3D

6. Flywheel pack (bench)

  • 1310 Hyper Hub (12 mm REX) on the Hogback's web: 4 x M4 x 10 socket heads.
  • 2 steel discs (3628-0032-0082) on each face of the Hogback: 8 x M4 x 20 thread-forming socket heads a side (check the first one's depth against the Hogback's core before driving all 16).

3D

7. Motor side plate: motor, latch ring, bearing

  • Press a 6801 into the -u side plate's 21 mm hole from the outer face.
  • Motor's face on the plate's INNER face, shaft out through the 18 mm hole: 4 x M4 x 12 button heads from outside.
  • Latch ring: 3 x M4 x 12 flat heads countersunk on the ring's inner face into 3 goBILDA 12 mm M4 standoffs, then 3 x M4 x 10 button heads through the plate into the standoffs (the ring sits 0.6 mm further out than drawn: no effect).

3D

8. The flywheel shaft stack

  • Cut the 240 mm 12 mm REX shaft (2109-4012-2400) to 222.5 mm; file the end.
  • Thread onto the shaft from the -u end, in order: [-u side plate's bearing comes later] 8 mm spacer, NECTAR tooth's -u bearing, 8 + 0.25 mm, lower half's -u bearing, 4 + 1 mm, top half's -u bearing, 12 + 8 + 6 + 1 + 0.5 mm, a 2910 collar, the flywheel pack (Hyper Hub to +u), 12 + 6 + 1 + 0.5 mm, top half's +u bearing, 4 + 1 mm, lower +u, 8 + 0.25 mm, NECTAR +u, 8 mm. Each hood piece's two plates take its two bearings, so the hood pieces go on as you reach them (top half innermost, NECTAR tooth outermost).
  • Before the -u collar is tightened: the top half's pins must sit in the lower half's slots and the pawl's track (turn the top half until the pin drops in), and the NECTAR tooth must rest on the base piece's support (it does once on the turret).

3D

9. Squishy wheel

  • The cast squishy wheel on the 216 mm 8 mm REX shaft, centred (wheel middle 8.3 mm toward +u from the shaft's middle), a 2920 collar against each face. Torque has to reach the wheel from the shaft: mount it the way your transfer's squishy wheels are mounted (lab question); the printed 95T has an 8 mm REX bore and goes on outside the +u plate in step 12.

3D

10. Onto the base: motor plate, shafts, servo plate

  • -u plate against the -u ear and buttress: 1 x M4 x 18 button head into the ear's nut, 2 x M4 x 30 into the buttress's nuts.
  • Flywheel stack: its -u end through the -u plate's bearing; squishy shaft through its 1611 bearing (flange outside).
  • +u plate (6801 + 1611 pressed in, servo window) over both shaft ends against the +u ear and buttress: 1 x M4 x 18 + 2 x M4 x 30.
  • Shims and collars: +u: 1.0 + 0.5 mm 12 mm shims, then a 2910 collar against the +u bearing's inner race. -u: a 2 + 1 mm spacer, then the HTD 24T pulley on its 1301 hub (step 10). Squishy shaft: a 2920 collar outside each plate. Spin the flywheel by hand: no rub.

3D

11. Servo + pinion

  • Hood at POLLEN lowest exit (servo 0): the lower half on its rest stops, the top half down against it.
  • Servo (25-3 Speed) commanded to its 0 end (the code's POLLEN-low position). 1906 hub on the spline, the acetal 30T pinion on the hub: 4 x M4 x 10. Its teeth must meet the sector's first teeth at this angle.
  • Servo onto 4 goBILDA 10 mm M4 standoffs on the +u plate's inner face (body out through the window): 4 x M4 x 8 through the ears, 4 x M4 x 12 through the plate. The pinion then sits 0.4 mm proud of the sector: fine.

3D

12. Belts

  • -u: HTD 24T on the motor (set screws on the REX flats, lined up by a straightedge with the shaft's 24T); 3411 24T bolted to the 1301 12 mm REX clamping hub (4 x M4 x 8) on the flywheel shaft; the 275 mm HTD 5 mm belt (exact at these centres: no tensioner).
  • +u: the HTD 3M 25T on the flywheel shaft (bought aluminium, or the printed fallback with 2 x M3 set screws on the REX flats); the printed 95T on the squishy shaft; the 444 mm HTD 3M belt (needs 443.9 mm: exact). A 2920 collar outside the 95T.

3D

  • Run the checks below (After assembly) on the bench first; then swap it onto the robot (step 1, then step 2 on the robot's 3208).

After assembly: checks and adjustments

CheckHow, and the adjustment
Squeeze, on real ballsFlywheel stopped, hood at POLLEN: push each of a few real POLLEN balls through by hand: firm and even. Built for 2.23 mm on the 72.53 mm mean ball (3.07 %), target 1-3 mm. Too loose: UHMW tape on the hood piece's ball band adds its thickness (0.1-0.13 mm a layer, edges along the ball's path). Too tight: sand the band. More than ~0.5 mm either way: re-print that hood piece with a new squeeze (one parameter). The same for NECTAR in NECTAR mode (built 4.15 mm on 92 mm).
Lateral gapsFeeler gauge between the POLLEN lower half's side teeth and the static NECTAR teeth, both sides: built 0.75 mm. Uneven: move 0.25-0.5 mm shims from one end of the flywheel stack to the other (step 8).
Rest stops and latchLower half on its stops (the base piece's cheeks): no rock. Latch: at servo 0 the pawl's hook sits 0.05 mm from the ring's end (sand the ring's end face or add tape until it just touches). Step the servo 0 -> 213 deg slowly: the pin lifts the lower half at 38, the pawl draws in, the NECTAR tooth is picked up at 182. Back down: no knock at 31-45 (the code slows there).
Servo zeroRe-check the pinion's mesh at both ends (POLLEN low, NECTAR high): no binding, no skipped tooth; 0.1 mm paper strip test.

Spin-upFlywheel by hand: no rub on the hood pieces (closest 2.3 mm to the steel discs). Then power: 1000, 2000, 3000 rpm, watch for wobble.
Motor ratioRead the 5203's label (1:1 assumed). Not 1:1: tell me; the speed loop and the calibration need its ratio.

Side stiffness: the buttresses

Sideways (along the axle) the two side plates bend as cantilevers off the base piece's ears. The two axles do not stiffen that: each sits in one ball bearing per plate, which passes no moment, so in sway they just move with the plates. Room before the axle's parts touch the base piece sideways: 0.294 / 0.308 mm in POLLEN mode, 0.312 mm in NECTAR mode (derived: the NECTAR tooth's edge on its support, the POLLEN side teeth inside the static NECTAR teeth). So each plate is tied to the base piece: a printed buttress (part of the base piece) on the plate's inside, from the ear up to 50 mm below the axle, the plate bolted to it with 2 x M4 x 30.
